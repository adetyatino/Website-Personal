Oil & Gas

HUGIN — Volve Field Data Lakehouse (North Sea)

Personal Project · Data Engineer · Apr 2026 – Present

An end-to-end pipeline over Equinor's open Volve field dataset, built with Apache Airflow, dbt, Apache Iceberg, and Trino on a bronze–silver–gold medallion architecture. A Kimball dimensional model with Slowly Changing Dimension Type 2 sits on top of six different source formats: production CSV, WITSML XML, LAS well logs, directional surveys, fixed-width ASCII, and reservoir simulation reports.

  • Identity-resolution crosswalk unifying well-naming variants from three source systems into canonical identifiers, with coverage reporting and explicit handling of unmapped records
  • Automated data quality tests (dbt tests and Soda Core) running in GitHub Actions on every pull request — grain validation, referential integrity, and domain business rules
  • Cross-grain reconciliation between daily and monthly production, flagging out-of-tolerance differences automatically

Banking & Fintech

BNPL Portfolio Quality & Early Warning

Independent Project · Data Analyst · Jun 2026 – Present

A credit-risk analytics pipeline (Python → DuckDB → dbt) with 18 layered models and 152 automated data quality tests, turning a 905K-row account-month panel and 527K transactions into 8 BI-ready data marts. Rate/mix/denominator decomposition shows the headline NPL drop from 2.59% to 2.41% (May–June 2026) came from receivables growth in the denominator, not from genuine portfolio improvement. The underlying data is synthetic, calibrated against the public OJK release of June 2026 — 7 of 7 metrics fall within a ±10% tolerance.

  • Vintage curves, DPD roll rates, segment risk, and geographic early warning across 160 regencies and cities
  • Limit-tightening and approval cut-off scenarios sized with Expected Loss = PD × EAD × LGD, showing the trade-off between avoided losses and forgone receivables
  • Power BI dashboard covering portfolio health, vintage, roll rate, segment risk, and geographic risk
